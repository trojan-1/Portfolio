About

The person behind the camera, and what's in the case.

TROJAN

I started out taking random shots back in school and the feeling of helping make memories last longer and telling a story never left me.Fast forward 4 years later, and I'm still behind the camera, chasing those beautiful moments, clean transitions and perfectly timed cuts. Only this time, with better gear, bigger ideas and more stories to tell. — behind the scenes, conferences, weddings and the occasional club.

I create videos, capture moments and bring stories to life through visuals. From events and weddings to brand films, documentaries and creative projects, I enjoy working with people who understand that a good video is more than just nice shots. It's about the story, the feeling and the memories it leaves behind.

For me, every project starts with a conversation. I want to understand the idea, the vision and, most importantly, how we want people to feel when they watch the final video. I love having a plan, but I also leave room for those unexpected moments that sometimes turn out to be the best shots.

My Creative Approach. I'm big on storytelling. I don't just want to point a camera at something and record what happens. I want to find the story in it. I shoot and edit myself, so the video stays a single point of view from script to delivery.For me, filmmaking isn't simply about capturing beautiful images. It's about creating something that makes people feel something.
